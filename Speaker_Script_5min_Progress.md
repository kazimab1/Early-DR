| 1 | Title | 0:15 | "I am working on early detection of diabetic retinopathy: telling grades 1 and 2 apart from healthy eyes, with calibrated probabilities, tested on data the model has not seen. This is where the project stands." |
| 2 | Progress at a glance | 1:00 | "Done: the data audit, the full Kaggle pipeline, three training arms with three seeds each, internal and APTOS evaluation, fourteen imbalance runs, three extensions and the diagnostic checks. | 3 | Key results so far | 1:15 | "On DDR all three arms reach sensitivity 0.93 at specificity 0.91, as designed. On held-out EyePACS patients the same frozen threshold behaves very differently: specificity 0.39 for DDR only, 0.89 for the source-balanced arm, and 0.013 for the original merge, which flags 99% of healthy images as disease. So the threshold does not transfer across datasets; compare AUROC, not sensitivity." |
| 4 | What the checks found | 1:00 | "Four percent of DDR test images had a near-duplicate in train; a group split removed it and results did not change. Image size alone predicts the label in APTOS at 0.96 but not in EyePACS, so APTOS is camera-confounded and EyePACS is the clean benchmark. Strong augmentation failed because of a BatchNorm mismatch; geometric augmentation on all classes works, but on minority classes only it leaks the label." |
| 5 | What these results do not show | 0:50 | "Read before quoting any number: the frozen threshold does not transfer, APTOS may carry other capture cues beyond size, same-patient leakage in DDR cannot be tested without patient IDs, and confidence intervals, ECE, Brier and five seeds are not reported yet. Post-hoc analyses are labelled." |
| 6 | Conclusions and next steps | 0:35 | "The merge as written teaches the source; source-balancing helps off-domain; EyePACS is the trustworthy benchmark. Next: five seeds on the group split, full reporting with intervals, own-source operating points, and a matched-subset check on APTOS." |
